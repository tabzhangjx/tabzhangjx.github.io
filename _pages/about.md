I'm Jiaxing Zhang, a Research Scientist at TikTok in Bellevue, WA.

I received my PhD in Informatics from New Jersey Institute of Technology in May 2025, where I was advised by [Prof. Hua Wei](https://www.public.asu.edu/~hwei27/) and [Prof. Michael Lee](https://people.njit.edu/profile/mjlee). I obtained my bachelor's degree in Computer Science (Honored Science Program) from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2020.

My research develops reliable and efficient learning systems for structured and multimodal data. Across my work, I study how models can preserve the right information, remain dependable under distribution shift, and adapt efficiently to specialized real-world tasks.

## Research

### Trustworthy and structured machine intelligence

I develop methods that make predictions and explanations more reliable when data has rich structure or shifts beyond the training distribution. My early work on code intelligence used program structure for vulnerability detection, variable-name recovery, and type inference. This led to a sustained research program in graph neural network explainability: information-bottleneck objectives and graph mixup for robust explanations, in-distribution proxy graphs, explanation methods for regression and 3D molecular graphs, and confidence-aware calibration. More recently, I have extended this structured perspective to graph-enhanced RAG and evidence-grounded language-model responses.

Selected work: **DeMinify**, **Commit-level NVDA**, **MixupExplainer**, **RegExplainer**, **In-Distributed Proxies**, **RISE**, **Confidence-Aware Explanation**, and **GE-Chat**.

### Efficient multimodal and video foundation models

I build multimodal foundation models that can be trained, adapted, and deployed efficiently for video understanding. This line spans end-to-end LVLM training and post-training, long-video temporal modeling, information-aware frame sampling, rationale-guided domain adaptation, mixture-of-experts routing, and scalable training infrastructure. The goal is to retain decision-critical visual evidence while reducing annotation, context, and compute costs in production-scale systems.

Selected work: **InfoShot** for shot-aware long-video sampling and **RB-FT** for rationale-bootstrapped adaptation of vision-language models.

The two directions share one central question: **how can a learning system preserve the information that matters while remaining robust, interpretable, and efficient under real-world constraints?**

I actively welcome research collaborations in trustworthy graph learning, GNN/LLM/VLM explainability, multimodal and video understanding, model architecture and training optimization, and scalable ML systems. I’m also open to working with motivated, well-prepared interns.
